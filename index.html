PROFILE

About Me

Dr. Abhishek Ray is an Assistant Professor in the Department of ECE at PDEU, Gandhinagar. He completed his Ph.D. at the National Institute of Technology Raipur with a CGPA of 9.0.

His doctoral research focused on “Design of Radiation Hardened FinFET for Integrated Circuit Applications”. His work spans semiconductor device modeling, radiation effects, FinFETs, analog and digital circuit design, layout, and MEMS-based energy harvesting.

Research Interests

Radiation-Hardened Devices

VLSI & Circuit Design
